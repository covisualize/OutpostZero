## Art and audio

- **Models:** generated by the scripts in `BlenderScripts/` with Blender 4.2 LTS. Blender's GPL covers Blender itself, not the files it produces.
- **Textures:** baked procedurally per asset by `BlenderScripts/texture_set.py`. No photo or third-party texture is shipped, CC0 or otherwise.
- **Audio:** every sound is synthesised at runtime by `ClipBook` (see `docs/AUDIO_ATTRIBUTION.md`). No recorded clip is shipped.
- **Fonts:** Unity's default runtime font.

When a third-party asset is added, list it here with its source, author and licence in the same change.
